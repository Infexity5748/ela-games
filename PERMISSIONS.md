# Publication basis

The original five packages use their retained published licences.

For the restored commercial games, the ELA Hub owner stated in this chat
that the rights holders gave permission in person to copy their game files
and publicly self-host them. The owner subsequently authorized publication.
This is an owner-reported authorization, not independently verified evidence
or a blanket open-source licence. Copyright and existing notices remain
with the original owners; see each game's PROVENANCE.md.

Operational tests establish neither ownership nor district-network access.
No proxy, network-filter evasion, fabricated authentication status, successful
advertising reward, or DRM/origin-lock workaround is introduced by this host.
